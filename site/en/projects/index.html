Around VCDesign

Related projects

Practices and tools around VCDesign. Each has its own norms in its own repository; they are not VCDesign specifications.

Status labels: consistent with v2 means no conflict with v2 vocabulary; v1 vocabulary means the material is still written in v1 terms. See the migration table to read v1 terms.

  • VC-AD

    Evolving in its own repository

    A development protocol for AI-driven coding in which decisions, contracts, connections and scope are handed to AI as YAML, so that humans keep the core of design and responsibility.

    "VCDesign is law; VC-AD is its implementation at the current technical level." (VCDesign README)

  • TDDD

    Independent practice record

    TODO Driven Development: an operating record of development in the generative-AI era that preserves decisions rather than code.

    tddd-playbook

  • Value Review

    v1 vocabulary

    A review sheet (L1–L4) for deciding, before implementation, which value is protected inside which boundary. In v2 the judgment gate and the canonical Review Output play this role.

    vcdesign-value-review

  • VMS / VMS Marketing

    v1 vocabulary

    An operating protocol that translates management and marketing concerns into loops, boundaries and decisions to design responsibility and value continuity.

    vms · vms-marketing

  • Re-derivation Layer

    Consistent with v2

    A layer that makes a robot's or machine's action explainable afterwards as responsibility and business meaning (physical AI and OT). The v2 glossary places it under "pass on".

  • Continuity Claim Protocol

    Consistent with v2

    A protocol for declaring identity, boundary, authority and signature before crossing into another organization or agent. Referenced by the boundary pattern's external_crossing.

  • idk-lamp.org

    Consistent with v2

    An everyday entry point: giving AI an "I don't know" lamp. It explains, with familiar stories, the idea that a guard which cannot decide stops and escalates as unknown.
